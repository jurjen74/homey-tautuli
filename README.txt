Tautulli for Homey brings your Plex Media Server activity into your smart home. Connect to Tautulli — a Plex monitoring tool — and automate your home around who is watching what, react to streams starting and stopping, and keep an eye on playback stats right from your Homey dashboard.

Multiple Tautulli servers are supported. Each is added as a separate device.

---

Requirements

- Tautulli v2.x running on your local network
- Plex Media Server connected to Tautulli
